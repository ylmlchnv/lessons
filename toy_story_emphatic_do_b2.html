1. Grammar recap / Повторение

At B2, the form is easy; the interesting part is why a speaker chooses it.

1) The basic pattern

do / does / did + base verb

I do like this plan.
Buzz does care about the others.
Woody did try to help.

Это утвердительные предложения. do/does/did добавляется не для времени, а для эмфазы.

2) Contrast / correction

We often use emphatic do when another person thinks the opposite.

“You don’t trust Buzz.”
“I do trust him.”

Смысл: «Нет, это не так. Я правда ему доверяю».

3) Strong feeling

I do love that idea.
Jessie does miss her friends.

Может усиливать мнение или чувство: «действительно», «правда», «всё-таки».

4) Present vs past

do → I/you/we/they
does → he/she/it
did → all subjects in the past

They do want to help.
He does know the way.
She did warn us.

5) Base verb after do/does/did

✅ He does care.
❌ He does cares.

✅ Woody did try.
❌ Woody did tried.

Время и лицо уже выражены в does/did, поэтому основной глагол идёт в базовой форме.

6) Not with BE or modals

✅ Buzz is brave.
❌ Buzz does be brave.

✅ Woody can help.
❌ Woody does can help.

С be и modal verbs emphatic do обычно не используется.

7) Tags can reinforce the point

You do trust him, don’t you?
She does remember, doesn’t she?
He did call, didn’t he?

8) Tone matters

I like the idea. = neutral

I do like the idea. = stronger; corrective, reassuring, or surprising

В речи do/does/did обычно получает ударение.

Neutral: Woody wants to help.
Correction: Woody does want to help. You’re wrong about him.
Past correction: Woody did try to help, even if the plan failed.
